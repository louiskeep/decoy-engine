Status: record

## What changed

- New `fileaccess/_protected_dir.py`: `protected_scratch_dir`, `open_protected_dir`, `ProtectedDirUnsafe`, `ProtectedDirAclFailed`, and the one live issued-root registry. POSIX is verified here; the Windows branch (DACL at `CreateDirectoryW`, DACL and owner read-back) is unverified until L0.1w.
- The quarantine arena validator `_private_dir` is replaced by `open_protected_dir`. Behavior change on POSIX: an empty permissive arena is tightened to 0700 and accepted; a non-empty permissive arena is refused (`arena_unsafe`).
- Reasons `runtime.scratch_unsafe` and `runtime.scratch_acl_failed`, registry 1.7.0, fixture `reason-manifest-v1.7-lib-l0-1.json`, `consumer-contract.json` regenerated, one `docs/reasons.md` token moved to 1.7.0.
- G-ONE-PROTECTED-DIR on the D9 registry: `dir_category` on every directory-creating row, `dir_test` on all but `protected_root`, guard in `tests/sentry/protected_dir/`.
