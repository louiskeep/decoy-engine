Notes on the mutations:

- E-T5 widens the critical section: the test monkeypatches the recorder's clock helper `_ns` with a
  0.5 ms `time.sleep` before the real conversion. Under the GIL the unlocked check-then-increment of
  `_emitted` does not interleave on its own; with the sleep inside it, two threads both pass the cap
  check. The `max_spans=256` cell checks each thread gets its own duration; the `max_spans=1` cell is
  the one M6 turns red.
- M8: inside `run_pipeline` the channel's own guard (`progress.py:196`) still swallows the re-raised
  error, so the red comes from the test's direct `on_event` and `close()` calls after the run. The
  recorder's own guard is what keeps a direct owner (Platform decorator `finally`, CLI exit path)
  from seeing the callback's exception.
- M1, M3 and M4 show the two layers: with only the recorder mutated, `SpanRecord.__post_init__`
  refuses (M1 shows that); M3 and M4 relax the vocabulary rule in the same scratch edit so the
  assertion on the record itself is what goes red.

## Proposed `docs/compatibility-contract.md` row for ER

| Surface | Contract |
|---|---|
| `decoy_engine.telemetry` (`SpanRecord`, `SpanRecorder`, `SPAN_NAMES`, `ATTRIBUTE_KEYS`, `RESOURCE_KEYS`, `TELEMETRY_SCHEMA`) | Schema `decoy-telemetry/v1`, pinned by `tests/fixtures/f30/telemetry-vocabulary.json` (E-T8). 13 span names, 12 attribute keys, 2 resource keys, closed string vocabularies for `decoy.mode`, `decoy.outcome`, `decoy.phase`, and rule vocabularies for `decoy.reason` (registered `namespace.code`), `error.type` (`safe_error_class` fixed point), `decoy.engine.version` (equals `__version__`). Additive only after GA; removing or renaming a name, key or vocabulary member is a schema bump. `SpanRecord` refuses any value outside them. `SpanRecorder(on_span, *, max_spans=256)` is a `ProgressSink`; it never raises into a run, a raising `on_span` is counted in `callback_failures`, `close()` is idempotent, `dropped` counts spans past the cap. The subpackage is the contract; nothing is added to `decoy_engine.__all__`. Pre-GA: hard delete. Registry stays `1.3.0` (read only). |

- Phase events emitted on engine worker threads reach no recorder (thread-local binding), `src/decoy_engine/progress.py:501-506` [4]

- `close()` stamps still-open phases with `time.time_ns()` at close, not at the failure, `src/decoy_engine/telemetry/_recorder.py:140` [8]
