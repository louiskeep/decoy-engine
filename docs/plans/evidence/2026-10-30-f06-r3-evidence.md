## Judgement calls a reviewer may want to contest

1. **Recovery identity.** `ReasonContext.object_ref` accepts at most 64 characters and the artifact ref is 65, so the F29 record carries the run identity and the exact artifact ref travels on `CleanupOutcome.ref` (attribute `declared_cast_cleanup` on the propagating exception). A platform adapter can reconcile by `(owner, run_id)` or by ref; `purge_expired` is the retention backstop.
2. **Fallback is evidence, not refusal, under `auto`.** The finding allowed either. The admission layers' fallback is the documented, accepted contract for `auto` (the capable full-frame route runs), so it is made machine-readable instead of rejected; a forced mode already rejects.
3. **A route is listed when it was on offer, not when it would have won.** The unified slice is listed for any declared job with the flag on and a mask table, even if its other admission checks would also have declined it; the statement "the contract kept it off" is true either way, and tying it to deeper checks would couple evidence to private heuristics.
4. **Multi-table split.** A declared job disables the split by design (`cast_run is None` gate). It has no capability refusal, so the registered, previously unused decision reason `declared_casting_full_frame` is what is carried.
5. **Physical declines in the corpus.** 78 admitted cells run on the fallback route by documented physical rules rather than on the nominal route. They are pinned in the sentry so growth is a reviewed decision.
