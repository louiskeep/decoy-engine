## Scope and known gaps

- The native companion is not installed here, so the compiled kernels are not exercised with typed declared columns; the oracle lanes and the Arrow-python dispatcher lane are. The companion-present CI job must run the route parity file.
- Refusals that are by design and registered: row quarantine on mixed and out-of-core; a declared relationship endpoint on chunked, out-of-core, mixed and unified-slice; streamed (sink) output of a declared table (it stays resident); the multi-table split is off for a declared job.
- `declared_casting_full_frame` stays in the append-only reason registry but is no longer emitted (routing no longer pins declared jobs to full-frame); its summary is historical.
- `out_of_core/_runner.py` is at the 700-line hard cap (no headroom); the decomposition target is in the census history.
- Consumer work, unchanged: CLI and Studio controls; an identity-bearing platform quarantine adapter; consumers that read CSV themselves must pass `lexeme_columns_by_table(config)` to their reader.
