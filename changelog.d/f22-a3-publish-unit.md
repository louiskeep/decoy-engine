### Added (publish_unit judge and bind, F22 a3, 2026-11-12)

`decoy_engine.publication.publish_unit(out, txn, request, tables=[...])` binds an `immutable_directory/v1` output unit under the destination lock: it reads the bound `_decoy_publication.json`, measures the staged Parquet tables itself (size, SHA-256, footer row count), judges, and only then writes the descriptor and publishes the `publication` stage durably. The same result already bound gives `already_committed` after the unit tree and its directory chain are fsynced; the same `operation_id` with a different result gives `operation_conflict`; a different result, files without a descriptor, or an unreadable, oversized or malformed descriptor is refused with `publication_target_occupied`, and the bound unit is never replaced or merged into. `publish_tables(out, {name: table}, request)` is the resident entry. Every exit is a value-free `CommitReceipt`; `raise_for_outcome` turns a receipt into `PublicationRefused`, `PublicationFailed` or `CommitUncertain`. The reason registry moves to 1.25.0 with thirteen `decoy.publication_*` codes, and `CommitUncertain`, `LockUnavailable`, `LockTimeout` and `LeafTooLarge` now map to registered reasons. A request carrying a vault is not accepted yet.
